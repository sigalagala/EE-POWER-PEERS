Getting Started

Your journey to becoming an Electrical Engineer starts here. We'll guide you through everything you need to know for a successful first year.

First Year Roadmap

Your first year in Electrical Engineering is designed to build a strong foundation. You'll explore core concepts that form the basis of all electrical systems and technologies.

Key Subjects You'll Encounter:

  • Circuit Analysis - Understand voltage, current, and resistance relationships
  • Electrical Measurements - Learn to use multimeters, oscilloscopes, and other tools
  • Digital Logic Design - Explore the building blocks of digital systems
  • Engineering Mathematics - Master the math behind electrical concepts
  • Electromagnetic Theory - Discover the principles of electromagnetism

Beyond academics, you'll also learn lab safety protocols, teamwork skills, and how to use essential engineering software like MATLAB and SPICE.

Know Your Essential Tools

Get familiar with your must-have Electrical Engineering tools. Understanding your tools early will make every lab session easier and more productive.

Digital Multimeter
Measures voltage, current, and resistance. Your most basic and essential tool.
Oscilloscope
Visualizes electrical signals. Critical for debugging circuits.
Power Supply
Provides adjustable voltage and current to power your circuits.
Breadboard
Allows temporary circuit construction without soldering.
Soldering Iron
Used to create permanent connections between components.
Wire Strippers
Removes insulation from wires for connections.
Function Generator
Produces various waveforms for testing circuits.
Simulation Software
SPICE, MATLAB, and other tools for virtual circuit testing.

Mentorship Program

Get paired with a senior mentor who'll help you adjust to campus life, study better, and discover your engineering passion. Mentorship builds confidence and support.

How Our Mentorship Works

Our peer-to-peer mentorship program connects you with experienced students who have successfully navigated the challenges you're about to face.

Benefits of Having a Mentor:

  • Personalized guidance on course selection and study strategies
  • Insider tips on professors, courses, and campus resources
  • Career advice and internship opportunities
  • Emotional support during stressful periods
  • Networking opportunities with other EE students

Mentor Matching Process:

  1. Complete our mentorship interest form
  2. We match you based on academic interests, career goals, and personality
  3. Attend an orientation session with your mentor
  4. Schedule regular meetings throughout the semester
